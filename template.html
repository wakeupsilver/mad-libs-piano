How it works

How to play

Pick a song, then press the green key. It sings the next word of the song. Keep pressing the green key and you'll play the whole song.

Press any other key and it sings a silly word instead, like "Twinkle, twinkle, little meatball."

Your song shows up under the piano as you play. When it's done, start over or try the next song.

Don't want to play? Press Auto play and watch it go.

On a computer, the letter keys play the piano too.
